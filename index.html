Biography

I am a Research Scientist at Meta. I received my Ph.D. in Oct 2024 from UCLA, where I was honored as a recipient of the Amazon Fellowship and the UCLA Dissertation Year Fellowship.

Currently, I architect industry-scale Multi-modal LLMs (MLLM) and LLM-native recommendation systems. My other research interests include representation learning, self-supervised learning, long-sequence modeling, and computational imaging.
